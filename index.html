How it works
  1. The app scans your video and finds every face.
  2. It groups faces by person. Tap the people you want to blur.
  3. Check the preview. Tap a face there to blur or unblur just that moment.
  4. Create the new video and save it. Your original isn't changed.

Your video is never uploaded. Face detection runs in this browser using the open-source face-api models included with this page. After the page has loaded it even works offline.

Face detection: face-api by Vladimir Mandic (MIT licence), built on TensorFlow.js.

Tips: short clips (under 1–2 minutes) work best on a phone. Keep the screen on while it works.
